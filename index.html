03 / Engineering approach

How I make
systems useful.

01

Frame before modeling.

Define the decision, unit of analysis, operational action, and cost of being wrong before touching the model.

02

Design for failure.

Idempotency, guardrails, deterministic state, and recovery paths are product features—not cleanup work.

03

Respect the constraint.

Sub-100ms inference, CPU-only models, DLP restrictions, or 150mA power budgets: constraints make the architecture honest.
